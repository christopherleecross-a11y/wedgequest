Wedge Quest

Answer your way around the ring. Collect all six wedges.

🎯 Goal of the game

Be the first to collect all six colored wedges — one from each category — and then answer a final question in the Hub to win.

  1. 🎲
    Roll and move either way around the ring.
  2. ❓
    Answer a question from the color you land on. Get it right and you go again; miss and the next player is up.
  3. ★
    Land on a wedge space (the ones with icon badges) and answer correctly to earn that wedge.
  4. 🏆
    Got all six? Head to the Hub and answer one last question to win!

Set any player to 🤖 to play against the computer. Easy gets about 45% of questions right, Medium 65%, Hard 85%.

    How to play
    • Roll, then tap one of the two glowing spaces (or use the arrow buttons).
    • Answer a question from that space's category. Right = roll again. Wrong = next player.
    • Spaces with an icon badge are wedge spaces. Answer correctly there to earn that wedge — it fills in a circle next to your name (hover or tap a circle to see its category).
    • Die spaces give you a free roll.
    • With all six wedges, head to the Hub and answer one final question to win.
    • 🤖 Computer players take their turns automatically.
    • Keyboard: 1–4 or A–D to answer, Enter to continue, R to roll.
