🧀 Wedge Quest

Answer your way around the ring and collect all six wedges.

Set any player to 🤖 Computer to play against the machine. Easy gets about 45% of questions right, Medium 65%, Hard 85%.

🎲
    How to play
    • Roll, then tap one of the two glowing spaces (clockwise or counter-clockwise).
    • Answer a question from that space's category. Get it right and you roll again; miss it and the turn passes.
    • The big ★ spaces are wedge spaces. A correct answer there earns that category's wedge.
    • 🎲 spaces give you a free roll.
    • Once you have all six wedges, head to the Hub and answer a final question to win.
    • 🤖 Computer players take their turns automatically.
    • Keyboard: 1–4 to answer, Enter to continue, R to roll.
    🏆

    All six wedges and a correct final answer. Well played!
