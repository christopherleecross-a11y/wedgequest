    🎯 Goal of the game

    Be the first to collect all six colored wedges — one from each category — and then answer a final question in the Hub to win.

    1. 🎲
      Everyone starts in the Hub. Roll and move the full count in any direction — along the spokes or around the ring — but you can't double back.
    2. ❓
      Answer a question from the color you land on. Get it right and you roll again; miss and the next player is up.
    3. ★
      Land on a wedge space (the big spaces with icon badges at the end of each spoke) and answer correctly to earn that wedge.
    4. ⭐
      Land in the Hub early? You pick any category for your question.
    5. 🏆
      Got all six? Land in the Hub by exact count. Your opponents choose the category of your final question — answer it to win!

      How to play
      • Everyone starts in the Hub. Roll, then tap any glowing space. You must move the full count, in any direction (spokes, ring or through the Hub), without doubling back.
      • Answer a question from that space's category. Right = roll again. Wrong = next player.
      • Spaces with an icon badge are wedge spaces. Answer correctly there to earn that wedge — it fills in a circle next to your name (hover or tap a circle to see its category).
      • Die spaces give you a free roll.
      • Land in the Hub before you have all six wedges and you choose the category.
      • With all six wedges, land in the Hub by exact count. Opponents pick the category of your final question. Miss it and you must leave the Hub and come back.
      • 🤖 Computer players take their turns automatically.
      • 🌐 Playing on separate devices? You can only roll, move and answer on your own turn — everyone watches the same board live.
      • Keyboard: R to roll, 1–4 or A–D to answer, Enter to continue.
